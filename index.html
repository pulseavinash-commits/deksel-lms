Scientific education for healthcare professionals. This site describes the physiology of muscle loss and the published pharmacology of nutritional ingredients (calcium β-hydroxy-β-methylbutyrate, L-leucine, whey protein isolate, soy protein isolate, vitamin D). The patients shown are illustrative composites, and the muscle character is a visual representation of muscle breakdown — not a measurement of any individual patient. This is not medical advice; nutritional support is an adjunct to disease-directed treatment, never a replacement.
