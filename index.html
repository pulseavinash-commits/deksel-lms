A scientific story, in segments

Muscle is a bank account.
Illness makes withdrawals.
Nothing on the chart defends it.

In diabetes, around surgery, and in rheumatological disease, muscle is actively broken down — window by window, through one pathway. Pick a segment below: three illustrated patient journeys, the 8-gram concept, and the five ingredients in depth.

Whatever the trigger — cytokines, cortisol, steroids, acidosis, bed rest — the end result is the same: breakdown up, building down, intake down.

And the climb back is never complete. The fall is steep and the recovery is flat: anabolic resistance raises the leucine threshold, the anabolic environment is impaired, and the next window opens before the last recovery has finished. Each tooth of the saw ends lower than the one before — hidden on the weighing scale, because fat quietly replaces the muscle.

Scientific education for healthcare professionals. This site describes the physiology of muscle loss and the published pharmacology of nutritional ingredients (calcium β-hydroxy-β-methylbutyrate, L-leucine, whey protein isolate, soy protein isolate, vitamin D). The patients shown are illustrative composites, and the muscle character is a visual representation of muscle breakdown — not a measurement of any individual patient. This is not medical advice; nutritional support is an adjunct to disease-directed treatment, never a replacement.
